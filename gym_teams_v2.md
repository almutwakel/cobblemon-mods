# Pokemon MMO — Gym Leader & Elite 4 Team Guide (v2)

> All Pokemon use **31 IVs across the board**. No Legendaries, no Mega Evolutions.
> Evolution rule: a Pokemon must be capable of existing at the gym's level cap.
> Players are scaled down to the gym's level cap when challenging it.

# MAIN GYMS (1–10)

---

## Gym 1 — Clay | Ground | Level Cap: 10 | Team Size: 3

**Strategy: Sand Stream Auto-Weather → Sand Rush Sweep**
Even at level 10, the Sand Stream + Sand Rush combo is devastating. Hippopotas sets sand on entry, Drilbur doubles its speed, and Wooper covers the Water weakness. At this level, most players won't have the tools to counter weather.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Hippopotas** | Sand Stream | Impish | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Stealth Rock, Yawn, Earthquake, Slack Off |
| **Drilbur** | Sand Rush | Jolly | Life Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Earthquake, Rock Slide, Swords Dance, Rapid Spin |
| **Wooper** | Water Absorb | Relaxed | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Scald, Earthquake, Recover, Toxic |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Hippopotas: Sand auto-sets. Stealth Rock turn 1. Yawn to force switches into hazards. Slack Off to stay healthy. Hippopotas is the anchor — never sacrifice it early.
- Drilbur: The win condition. Sand Rush doubles speed in sand. Swords Dance if the opponent is slower or forced out by Yawn. After +2 Atk, Earthquake sweeps most things available at level 10. Rock Slide covers Flying types and has flinch chance.
- Wooper: Water Absorb shuts down Water-type attacks the player brings to counter Ground. Scald has 30% burn chance. Eviolite bulk is surprisingly high. Toxic wears down anything that tries to wall it.

## Gym 2 — Gardenia | Grass | Level Cap: 15 | Team Size: 3

**Strategy: Prankster Disable + Contrary Leaf Storm + Spore**
Cottonee is the star — Prankster gives all status moves priority. Servine's Contrary turns Leaf Storm's SpA drop into a +2 boost. Shroomish carries Spore for guaranteed sleep. Three Pokemon, three devastating gimmicks.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Cottonee** | Prankster | Bold | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Leech Seed, Encore, Stun Spore, Giga Drain |
| **Servine** | Contrary | Timid | Life Orb | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Leaf Storm, Glare, Giga Drain, Hidden Power Fire |
| **Shroomish** | Effect Spore | Jolly | Focus Sash | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Spore, Bullet Seed, Drain Punch, Rock Tomb |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Cottonee: Prankster Leech Seed first turn (goes before ANY attack). Encore on the second turn — this locks the opponent into whatever they just used. If they used a setup move, they're stuck repeating it while Leech Seed drains them. Stun Spore against anything that switches in. This Pokemon controls the entire early game.
- Servine: Spam Leaf Storm. Each use RAISES SpA by 2 stages with Contrary. After two Leaf Storms, Servine is at +4 SpA and obliterating everything. Glare paralyzes even Grass-types (unlike Stun Spore). HP Fire covers Bug/Steel.
- Shroomish: Spore is 100% accurate sleep. Bullet Seed breaks Substitutes and Focus Sashes (multi-hit). Focus Sash guarantees Shroomish gets at least one Spore off. Effect Spore punishes contact moves with a 30% chance of sleep/poison/paralysis.

## Gym 3 — Korrina | Fighting | Level Cap: 20 | Team Size: 4

**Strategy: Speed Boost Protect Stall → Sweep + Regenerator Pivoting**
Combusken with Speed Boost becomes untouchable after two Protects. Mienfoo pivots endlessly with Regenerator, healing a third of its HP on every switch. At level 20, most players lack the tools to handle Speed Boost.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Mienfoo** | Regenerator | Jolly | Eviolite | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | U-Turn, Drain Punch, Knock Off, Fake Out |
| **Combusken** | Speed Boost | Adamant | Life Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Protect, Swords Dance, Blaze Kick, Sky Uppercut |
| **Croagunk** | Dry Skin | Adamant | Eviolite | 252 Atk / 128 HP / 128 Spe | Drain Punch, Sucker Punch, Poison Jab, Fake Out |
| **Pancham** | Iron Fist | Adamant | Eviolite | 252 Atk / 252 HP / 4 Def | Drain Punch, Crunch, Parting Shot, Comet Punch |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Mienfoo: Fake Out for free chip turn 1. U-Turn into Combusken on turn 2. Regenerator heals 33% on every switch — Mienfoo comes back repeatedly throughout the fight. Knock Off removes opponent items (devastating against Eviolite users the player likely has at this level).
- Combusken: Protect turn 1 after entering → gain Speed Boost. Swords Dance turn 2 if not threatened. After +2 Atk and +1 Speed, Blaze Kick sweeps. Fire/Fighting coverage is nearly unresisted at level 20. If the player's counter is slower, Combusken snowballs.
- Croagunk: Dry Skin heals from Water moves — counters the Water-types players bring for Fighting. Sucker Punch is priority Dark coverage that hits Psychic and Ghost types before they can move. Fake Out + Drain Punch for sustain.
- Pancham: Parting Shot lowers BOTH offensive stats and switches out — feeds momentum to Combusken. Iron Fist boosts Drain Punch and Comet Punch. Crunch for Ghost/Psychic.

## Gym 4 — Byron | Steel | Level Cap: 25 | Team Size: 5

**Strategy: Hazard Stacking + Leech Seed + Priority Revenge**
Skarmory and Ferroseed create an oppressive hazard and chip damage core. Scizor cleans with Technician Bullet Punch. At level 25, the sheer bulk of Steel types with Eviolite is overwhelming.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Skarmory** | Sturdy | Impish | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Spikes, Whirlwind, Roost, Brave Bird |
| **Ferroseed** | Iron Barbs | Relaxed | Eviolite | 252 HP / 128 Def / 128 SpD | Stealth Rock, Leech Seed, Thunder Wave, Gyro Ball |
| **Scizor** | Technician | Adamant | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 HP / 4 Spe | Bullet Punch, U-Turn, Superpower, Pursuit |
| **Metang** | Clear Body | Adamant | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 Def | Meteor Mash, Zen Headbutt, Bullet Punch, Ice Punch |
| **Pawniard** | Defiant | Adamant | Eviolite | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Sucker Punch, Knock Off, Iron Head, Swords Dance |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Skarmory: Spikes turn 1, layering up to 3. Whirlwind forces switches into hazards repeatedly. Sturdy prevents any OHKO. Roost keeps it alive for multiple cycles. This is the grinding wheel.
- Ferroseed: Stealth Rock + Leech Seed + Iron Barbs creates a chip damage nightmare. Any physical attacker touching Ferroseed takes Iron Barbs recoil AND feeds Leech Seed. Thunder Wave cripples anything fast. Gyro Ball does massive damage to fast opponents (Ferroseed's abysmal speed makes Gyro Ball hit 150 BP against most things).
- Scizor: Choice Band Technician Bullet Punch is one of the best revenge kills in the game. STAB + Technician + Choice Band on a 40 BP priority move. U-Turn for scouting and momentum. Pursuit traps fleeing Pokemon.
- Metang: Clear Body prevents stat drops. Eviolite bulk is massive. Meteor Mash has a 20% Attack boost chance. Bullet Punch backup priority.
- Pawniard: Defiant punishes ANY stat drop with +2 Atk — players who use Intimidate or Defog feed it. Sucker Punch is priority Dark STAB. Swords Dance on a free turn makes it lethal.

## Gym 5 — Blaine | Fire | Level Cap: 30 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Drought Sun Team — Auto-Weather + Flash Fire / Solar Power**
Ninetales sets permanent sun. Fire moves hit 50% harder, Water moves weaken by 50%. Solar Beam requires no charge. The team is designed so every Pokemon benefits from sun in a different way, making weather control the ONLY answer.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Ninetales** | Drought | Timid | Heat Rock | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Fire Blast, Solar Beam, Will-O-Wisp, Nasty Plot |
| **Arcanine** | Intimidate | Adamant | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Flare Blitz, Extreme Speed, Wild Charge, Close Combat |
| **Charmeleon** | Solar Power | Timid | Eviolite | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Fire Blast, Dragon Pulse, Solar Beam, Focus Blast |
| **Houndoom** | Flash Fire | Timid | Life Orb | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Nasty Plot, Dark Pulse, Fire Blast, Solar Beam |
| **Centiskorch** | Flash Fire | Adamant | Heavy-Duty Boots | 252 Atk / 252 HP / 4 Spe | Fire Lash, Leech Life, Coil, Power Whip |
| **Torkoal** | Drought | Bold | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpA | Rapid Spin, Lava Plume, Yawn, Stealth Rock |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Ninetales: Drought sets sun immediately. Will-O-Wisp physical attackers. Nasty Plot if safe. Solar Beam covers Water/Rock/Ground with no charge in sun. Heat Rock extends sun to 8 turns.
- Arcanine: Intimidate drops opponent Attack on entry. Choice Band Extreme Speed is +2 priority — outspeeds all other priority moves except Fake Out. Sun-boosted Flare Blitz from Choice Band is devastating.
- Charmeleon: Solar Power boosts SpA by 50% in sun (costs HP each turn). With Eviolite sustain, it survives to fire off several sun-boosted, Solar Power-boosted Fire Blasts. Solar Beam for Water types.
- Houndoom: Flash Fire absorbs Fire attacks aimed at it — switching in on a predicted Fire move gives a free power boost stacking with sun. Nasty Plot + Dark Pulse + Fire Blast.
- Centiskorch: Flash Fire for a second Fire absorber. Fire Lash lowers the target's Defense each hit. Coil boosts Attack, Defense, and Accuracy. Power Whip covers Water/Rock.
- Torkoal: Backup Drought setter if Ninetales falls and sun expires. Yawn forces switches into Stealth Rock. Lava Plume has 30% burn chance. Rapid Spin removes hazards the player sets.

## Gym 6 — Roxie | Poison | Level Cap: 35 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Toxic Spikes + Speed Boost Lead + Offensive Poison**
Without Toxapex or Amoonguss at this level, Roxie plays aggressive Poison instead of stall. Scolipede sets Toxic Spikes at blistering speed, Gengar abuses SubDisable, and Galarian Weezing's Neutralizing Gas disables ALL abilities on the field.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Scolipede** | Speed Boost | Jolly | Focus Sash | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Toxic Spikes, Megahorn, Poison Jab, Swords Dance |
| **Weezing-Galar** | Neutralizing Gas | Bold | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Strange Steam, Will-O-Wisp, Pain Split, Taunt |
| **Gengar** | Cursed Body | Timid | Black Sludge | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Substitute, Disable, Shadow Ball, Sludge Wave |
| **Nidoking** | Sheer Force | Modest | Life Orb | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Earth Power, Sludge Wave, Ice Beam, Thunderbolt |
| **Crobat** | Infiltrator | Jolly | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Brave Bird, U-Turn, Cross Poison, Defog |
| **Salazzle** | Corrosion | Timid | Focus Sash | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Toxic, Flamethrower, Sludge Wave, Encore |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Scolipede: Toxic Spikes turn 1 (two layers if possible — second layer upgrades Toxic from regular poison to badly poisoned). Speed Boost gains +1 Spe per turn. Focus Sash guarantees survival. After spikes are down, Swords Dance + Megahorn sweeps or pivots out.
- Galarian Weezing: Neutralizing Gas removes ALL abilities while Weezing is on the field — Guts doesn't work, Regenerator doesn't work, Magic Guard doesn't work. This counters the player's ability-dependent strategies. Will-O-Wisp burns physical attackers. Strange Steam has 20% confusion chance. Taunt stops opposing hazards and healing.
- **Gengar SubDisable trap:** Substitute → opponent attacks the Sub → Disable locks out that move. Now they can't use their main STAB. Cursed Body adds a 30% chance to disable contact moves. Shadow Ball + Sludge Wave provide dual STAB.
- Nidoking: Sheer Force + Life Orb on qualifying moves means massive damage with NO Life Orb recoil. Earth Power, Ice Beam, Thunderbolt, Sludge Wave — perfect four-move coverage that hits almost everything super-effectively.
- Crobat: Fastest non-boosted Pokemon on the team. Infiltrator ignores Substitute. Choice Band Brave Bird chunks anything. U-Turn keeps momentum.
- Salazzle: Corrosion lets Toxic hit Steel and Poison types — the only Pokemon that can do this. Encore locks setup sweepers into their boosting move. Focus Sash guarantees at least one Toxic lands.

## Gym 7 — Crasher Wake | Water | Level Cap: 40 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Drizzle Rain Team — Swift Swim Sweepers**
Pelipper sets rain automatically. Every Swift Swim user doubles their speed. Water moves hit 50% harder, Fire weakens by 50%. This is one of the most proven competitive archetypes in Pokemon history.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Pelipper** | Drizzle | Bold | Damp Rock | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpA | Scald, U-Turn, Roost, Defog |
| **Kingdra** | Swift Swim | Modest | Life Orb | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Hydro Pump, Draco Meteor, Hurricane, Flash Cannon |
| **Barraskewda** | Swift Swim | Adamant | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Liquidation, Close Combat, Poison Jab, Aqua Jet |
| **Kabutops** | Swift Swim | Adamant | Life Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Swords Dance, Waterfall, Stone Edge, Aqua Jet |
| **Swampert** | Torrent | Adamant | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 Def | Stealth Rock, Earthquake, Ice Punch, Flip Turn |
| **Gastrodon** | Storm Drain | Bold | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Scald, Earth Power, Recover, Toxic |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Pelipper: Drizzle sets rain. U-Turn immediately into a Swift Swim sweeper. Re-enter later to refresh rain if it's overwritten. Roost for sustain. Damp Rock extends rain to 8 turns.
- Kingdra: Swift Swim in rain outspeeds almost everything. Rain-boosted Hydro Pump is 202.5 effective BP (base 110 × 1.5 STAB × 1.5 rain × 0.818 because I should simplify — just say it's absurdly powerful). Draco Meteor nukes Dragon-resistant Water counters. Hurricane never misses in rain.
- Barraskewda: Choice Band + Swift Swim. The fastest physical Water sweeper. Liquidation in rain is overwhelming. Close Combat hits Ferrothorn, the classic rain counter.
- Kabutops: Swords Dance behind a switch. Rain-boosted Waterfall + Stone Edge coverage. Aqua Jet priority insurance if rain ends.
- Swampert: Only weak to Grass (4x). Stealth Rock support. Flip Turn maintains momentum like U-Turn but benefits from rain. Ice Punch for Grass switch-ins.
- Gastrodon: Storm Drain absorbs and gets a SpA boost from all Water attacks — hard counters opposing Water types and protects the team from Water moves. Immune to Electric via Ground typing. Recover + Toxic to stall.

## Gym 8 — Sabrina | Psychic | Level Cap: 45 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Future Sight + Teleport Pivoting + Trick Room**
Slowbro uses Future Sight (landing in 2 turns), then Teleports out to bring in a sweeper for free — the sweeper attacks AND Future Sight lands simultaneously. Reuniclus and Hatterene set Trick Room. Magic Bounce reflects all status.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Slowbro** | Regenerator | Bold | Heavy-Duty Boots | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpA | Future Sight, Teleport, Scald, Slack Off |
| **Reuniclus** | Magic Guard | Quiet | Life Orb | 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD | Trick Room, Psyshock, Focus Blast, Shadow Ball |
| **Hatterene** | Magic Bounce | Quiet | Life Orb | 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD | Trick Room, Psychic, Dazzling Gleam, Mystical Fire |
| **Alakazam** | Magic Guard | Timid | Life Orb | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Nasty Plot, Psychic, Shadow Ball, Focus Blast |
| **Bronzong** | Levitate | Sassy | Leftovers | 252 HP / 128 Def / 128 SpD | Stealth Rock, Trick Room, Gyro Ball, Toxic |
| **Medicham** | Pure Power | Jolly | Choice Scarf | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | High Jump Kick, Zen Headbutt, Ice Punch, Bullet Punch |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Slowbro: Future Sight turn 1 (120 BP Psychic attack lands in 2 turns). Teleport on turn 2 — negative priority move guarantees the incoming ally takes zero damage on the switch. Regenerator heals 33% on every switch.
- **The Future Sight combo:** A sweeper enters for free via Teleport → attacks immediately → Future Sight lands on the same turn. Two attacks in one turn. Almost nothing survives. Slowbro re-enters, sets another Future Sight, Teleports again. Repeat.
- Reuniclus: Trick Room setter. Magic Guard means Life Orb costs no HP. In Trick Room, Reuniclus's low speed becomes an advantage. Calm Mind stacks make it progressively unkillable.
- Hatterene: Magic Bounce reflects status moves (Stealth Rock, Toxic, Thunder Wave, Spore) back at the sender. Also sets Trick Room. Players who rely on status get destroyed.
- Alakazam: The fast-mode sweeper outside Trick Room. Magic Guard + Life Orb = free 30% power boost. Nasty Plot + three coverage moves.
- Bronzong: Steel/Psychic with Levitate — only Fire and Dark hit super-effectively. Backup Trick Room. Gyro Ball deals massive damage to fast targets in Trick Room.
- Medicham: Pure Power doubles base Attack. Choice Scarf outspeeds unboosted threats. High Jump Kick is nuclear-powered but risky (crash damage on miss — the AI should use it wisely against targets unlikely to Protect or switch to Ghost).

## Gym 9 — Drayden | Dragon | Level Cap: 50 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Dragon Dance Relay — Three Setup Sweepers With Overlapping Coverage**
Three Dragon Dance users that cover each other's checks. Garchomp handles hazards and Swords Dance. Haxorus's Mold Breaker ignores protective abilities. The player must answer three different boosting threats — running out of answers means losing.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Garchomp** | Rough Skin | Jolly | Rocky Helmet | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Stealth Rock, Swords Dance, Earthquake, Scale Shot |
| **Salamence** | Moxie | Jolly | Life Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Dragon Dance, Dual Wingbeat, Earthquake, Dragon Claw |
| **Haxorus** | Mold Breaker | Adamant | Choice Scarf | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Outrage, Close Combat, Earthquake, First Impression |
| **Kommo-o** | Bulletproof | Impish | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Stealth Rock, Body Press, Dragon Tail, Toxic |
| **Goodra** | Sap Sipper | Calm | Assault Vest | 252 HP / 252 SpD / 4 SpA | Draco Meteor, Sludge Bomb, Flamethrower, Thunderbolt |
| **Kingdra** | Sniper | Modest | Scope Lens | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Focus Energy, Draco Meteor, Hydro Pump, Ice Beam |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Garchomp: Stealth Rock turn 1. Swords Dance on switches. Scale Shot hits 2-5 times, raises speed, lowers defense — perfect cleanup move. Rocky Helmet + Rough Skin = 29% recoil on every physical contact move. Opponents touching Garchomp get punished severely.
- Salamence: Dragon Dance + Moxie. One DD makes it fast. Each KO from Moxie raises Attack. This snowballs an entire team if the player doesn't have an Ice/Fairy type ready. Dual Wingbeat hits twice (breaks Substitute and Focus Sash).
- Haxorus: Choice Scarf for surprise speed — players expecting it to be slow get caught. Mold Breaker ignores Sturdy (Stone Edge KOs through it), Levitate (Earthquake hits), and Disguise. First Impression is 90 BP priority Bug move on the first turn only.
- Kommo-o: Bulletproof makes it immune to Shadow Ball, Aura Sphere, Sludge Bomb, Energy Ball, and all ball/bomb moves. Defensive pivot with Dragon Tail phazing into Stealth Rock.
- Goodra: Assault Vest special tank. Sap Sipper absorbs Grass moves for an Attack boost. Full special coverage handles anything the physical dragons can't.
- **Kingdra Crit Build:** Focus Energy guarantees critical hits. Sniper boosts crit damage to 2.25x. Crits ignore the opponent's defensive boosts AND ignore Draco Meteor's SpA drop. After Focus Energy, every hit is a crit.

## Gym 10 — Morty | Ghost | Level Cap: 55 | Team Size: 6

**Strategy: Prankster Burn + SubDisable Trap + Stance Change + Disguise Setup**
The final main gym. Sableye burns everything with priority. Gengar traps with Substitute + Disable. Aegislash stance-dances between fortress defense and nuclear offense. This team is designed to frustrate, trap, and outlast.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Sableye** | Prankster | Bold | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Will-O-Wisp, Recover, Knock Off, Taunt |
| **Gengar** | Cursed Body | Timid | Black Sludge | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Substitute, Disable, Shadow Ball, Focus Blast |
| **Aegislash** | Stance Change | Brave | Weakness Policy | 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD | King's Shield, Swords Dance, Shadow Sneak, Sacred Sword |
| **Dusclops** | Pressure | Bold | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Will-O-Wisp, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Night Shade |
| **Mimikyu** | Disguise | Adamant | Life Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Swords Dance, Shadow Sneak, Play Rough, Shadow Claw |
| **Annihilape** | Defiant | Adamant | Leftovers | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Bulk Up, Drain Punch, Rage Fist, Shadow Claw |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Sableye: Prankster Will-O-Wisp before ANY attack — halves physical attackers' damage via burn. Taunt shuts down opposing setup, hazards, and healing. Knock Off removes items. Recover sustains indefinitely. Only Fairy types threaten Sableye.
- **Gengar SubDisable trap:** Substitute → opponent attacks the Sub → Disable that move. Now they can't use their primary STAB. Repeat until they struggle or are forced to switch. Cursed Body adds a 30% passive Disable chance on contact. Shadow Ball + Focus Blast for damage.
- Aegislash: Shield Forme has base 150 Def/SpD. Attacks switch it to Blade Forme (base 150 Atk/SpA). King's Shield reverts to Shield Forme AND drops the opponent's Attack by 2 if they make contact. **Weakness Policy play:** deliberately take a super-effective hit in Shield Forme (survives due to 150 defenses) → +2 Atk/SpA → Swords Dance → Shadow Sneak priority at +4 Atk ends games.
- Dusclops: Eviolite makes Dusclops BULKIER than Dusknoir. Pressure doubles PP usage on the opponent. Pain Split averages HP — devastates high-HP attackers. Seismic Toss and Night Shade deal fixed level-based damage. This Pokemon stalls forever and drains PP.
- Mimikyu: Disguise blocks one hit for free. Guaranteed Swords Dance. Shadow Sneak priority at +2 Atk cleans up weakened teams. Play Rough for Dark types.
- **Annihilape:** Rage Fist starts at 50 BP and increases by 50 for EACH time Annihilape is hit — after taking 3 hits it's 200 BP Ghost STAB. Bulk Up raises Attack and Defense. Drain Punch heals. The longer this thing stays alive, the scarier it gets.

## Viola — Bug | Level 60

**Strategy: Sticky Web + Quiver Dance + U-Turn Momentum**
Galvantula guarantees Sticky Web with Compound Eyes. Volcarona is the centerpiece — Quiver Dance boosts SpA, SpD, and Speed simultaneously. The team chains U-Turn to always maintain switch advantage while Sticky Web slows the opponent's entire squad.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Galvantula** | Compound Eyes | Timid | Focus Sash | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Sticky Web, Thunder, Bug Buzz, Volt Switch |
| **Volcarona** | Flame Body | Timid | Heavy-Duty Boots | 252 SpA / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Quiver Dance, Bug Buzz, Fire Blast, Giga Drain |
| **Scizor** | Technician | Adamant | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 HP / 4 Spe | Bullet Punch, U-Turn, Superpower, Knock Off |
| **Heracross** | Guts | Adamant | Flame Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Close Combat, Megahorn, Knock Off, Facade |
| **Araquanid** | Water Bubble | Brave | Assault Vest | 252 HP / 252 Atk / 4 SpD | Liquidation, Lunge, Mirror Coat, Sticky Web |
| **Orbeetle** | Frisk | Bold | Light Clay | 252 HP / 252 Def / 4 SpD | Reflect, Light Screen, U-Turn, Psychic |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Galvantula: Sticky Web turn 1 (all grounded opponents lose 1 speed stage on entry for the entire match). Focus Sash guarantees survival. Compound Eyes Thunder has 91% accuracy. Volt Switch for momentum after web is up.
- Volcarona: The win condition. Quiver Dance once behind screens → sweep. Flame Body burns attackers on contact. Heavy-Duty Boots ignores Stealth Rock (Volcarona's 4x weakness). After one Quiver Dance, very little outspeeds or survives its attacks.
- Scizor: Technician Bullet Punch is the premium priority move. Choice Band U-Turn on Sticky Web'd opponents generates huge momentum. Knock Off strips items.
- Heracross: Guts + Flame Orb. Immune to other status, 50% Attack boost. Megahorn + Close Combat coverage is almost unresisted. Facade hits 140 BP while statused.
- Araquanid: Water Bubble doubles Water move damage AND halves Fire damage taken. Liquidation hits as hard as Hydro Pump. Mirror Coat reflects special attacks for double damage.
- Orbeetle: Dual screens (Reflect + Light Screen) to enable Volcarona setup. Frisk reveals opponent items. U-Turn to pivot after screens.

## Cheren — Normal | Level 60

**Strategy: CurseLax + Belly Drum + Extreme Speed Priority Chain**
Normal's strength is versatility and priority. Three Pokemon carry Extreme Speed or similar priority. Snorlax with Curse becomes a progressively unkillable monster. Ditto copies the player's strongest Pokemon at full health.

| Pokemon | Ability | Nature | Item | EVs | Moves |
|---------|---------|--------|------|-----|-------|
| **Snorlax** | Thick Fat | Careful | Leftovers | 252 HP / 252 SpD / 4 Atk | Curse, Body Slam, Rest, Sleep Talk |
| **Zangoose** | Toxic Boost | Jolly | Toxic Orb | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Facade, Close Combat, Knock Off, Quick Attack |
| **Staraptor** | Reckless | Jolly | Choice Scarf | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Brave Bird, Double-Edge, Close Combat, U-Turn |
| **Diggersby** | Huge Power | Adamant | Choice Band | 252 Atk / 252 Spe / 4 HP | Earthquake, Return, Fire Punch, Quick Attack |
| **Porygon2** | Download | Quiet | Eviolite | 252 HP / 252 SpA / 4 SpD | Trick Room, Tri Attack, Ice Beam, Thunderbolt |
| **Ditto** | Imposter | Relaxed | Choice Scarf | 252 HP / 128 Def / 128 SpD | Transform | — | — | — |

**AI Behavior:**
- Lead Porygon2: Download boosts whichever offensive stat corresponds to the opponent's lower defensive stat. Trick Room reverses speed. Eviolite Porygon2 is one of the bulkiest Pokemon in the game. Tri Attack has a 20% chance of burn, freeze, or paralysis (boosted to 40% with Serene Grace... but Download is better).
- Snorlax: CurseLax. Curse boosts Attack and Defense. Rest + Sleep Talk for infinite recovery. After 2-3 Curses, Snorlax has enormous bulk AND power. Thick Fat resists Fire and Ice. In Trick Room, Snorlax moves first. This can solo teams if not handled.
- Zangoose: Toxic Boost + Toxic Orb = same as Guts but for Zangoose specifically. Facade is 140 BP + STAB when statused. Close Combat for Steel/Rock. One of the hardest-hitting Normal types.

# DESIGN NOTES

**Difficulty Curve:**
- Lv 10–15 (Gyms 1–2): Teams of 3 with one core gimmick. Teach the player about weather and status.
- Lv 20–25 (Gyms 3–4): Teams of 4-5 with layered strategies. Require type knowledge and team building.
- Lv 30–40 (Gyms 5–7): Full teams of 6 with proven competitive archetypes (Sun, Poison Offense, Rain).
- Lv 45–55 (Gyms 8–10): Near-tournament-level teams. Future Sight combos, Dragon Dance relays, Ghost traps.
- Lv 60 (Rotating): Endgame challenges. Each one represents a complete, tournament-viable archetype. Players should have a deep roster to handle all 9.
- Lv 65 (Elite 4): Endurance gauntlet. Resource management across 4 consecutive fights is the real challenge.

**Anti-Cheese Measures Across All Gyms:**
- Haze / Clear Smog / Unaware appear throughout to punish pure setup strategies.
- Priority moves on most teams prevent speed-boosting from auto-winning.
- Mixed attackers prevent single-wall strategies.
- Knock Off is everywhere to punish item reliance.
- Ditto (Cheren, Oak) copies and outspeeds the player's own boosted sweeper.
- Multiple teams carry sleep, paralysis, and burn to tax the player's status recovery.
- Galarian Weezing (Roxie) disables ALL abilities.
- Magic Bounce (Sabrina) reflects hazards and status back.

**Elite Four Endurance Design:**
- E4 #1 (Weather): Moderate difficulty. Forces the player to use some resources.
- E4 #2 (Hyper Offense): High burst difficulty. Demands the player's best revenge killers.
- E4 #3 (Stall): Low kill threat but massive PP/resource drain. The "trap" fight.
- E4 #4 (Dragons): Maximum threat against a weakened team. The player pays for any waste in fights 1-3.
